## Product Types

We support seven product types to cover every kind of purchase you might need. Here is what each means in plain language:

### 1. Simple Products

A straightforward product with one option and one price. For example: a single bottle of shampoo, a specific toy, or a bag of treats.

- One price, one SKU.
- Add to cart and check out — no extra choices needed.

### 2. Variable Products

A product that comes in multiple variations, such as different sizes, flavours, or strengths. For example: flea treatment in small dog (0–5 kg), medium dog (5–15 kg), and large dog (15+ kg) variants.

- You choose the variant that matches your pet on the product page.
- Each variant has its own price and stock level.
- Price changes based on the variant you select.

### 3. Grouped Products

A collection of related products displayed together on one page. For example: a "Puppy Starter Kit" that lists a bed, a bowl, and a toy — each added to your cart individually.

- Each item in the group is a separate product added to your cart as its own line.
- You can choose which items from the group you want and in what quantities.
- The group display is for convenience; pricing is per individual item.

### 4. External / Affiliate Products

Products that we recommend but that are purchased through a partner website. For example: pet insurance policies or specialised equipment.

- These are not purchasable through our checkout.
- You will see a link or button to visit the partner's website.
- No cart, no checkout, no stock tracking on our side.

### 5. Virtual Products

Products with no physical item to ship. For example: a digital gift certificate or a service add-on.

- No shipping weight — these items are excluded from shipping calculations.
- Delivered digitally or applied to your account.

### 6. Downloadable Products

Digital files you can download after purchase. For example: post-operative care sheets, travel certificates, or educational guides.

- After payment, you receive a secure download link.
- Access may be limited to a certain number of downloads or a time window.
- No physical item is shipped.

### 7. Composite Products (Bundles)

Customisable bundles where you build your own combination. For example: a "Wellness Bundle" where you pick a food bag, a supplement, and a treat — all in one product.

- The product page shows slots you fill with your preferred options.
- Some slots may be required (you must choose at least one option) and some may be optional.
- The total price is either a fixed bundle price or the sum of the options you select.
- Prescription requirements are derived from the options you choose — if any option requires a prescription, the entire bundle requires approval.

When you check out, you choose how you want to receive your order:

### Ship to Door

- Your order is packed and shipped to the delivery address you provide.
- Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the weight of your items and your delivery location.
- Tracking information is provided once your order dispatches.

- Collect your order from the clinic at no additional cost.

## Subscriptions and Auto-Refill

For products you need regularly — such as monthly flea and tick preventatives or therapeutic diets — you can set up a subscription.

### How Subscriptions Work

- Choose a subscription frequency: monthly, quarterly, or another interval.
- Your order is automatically placed and billed on schedule.
- You receive a notification before each order so you are never surprised by a charge.
- You can pause, resume, or cancel your subscription at any time through your account.

### What Can Be Subscribed

- Subscriptions are available for physical products (simple and variable types).
- Virtual, downloadable, grouped, composite, and external products are not available for subscription.
- Prescription items on subscription require an active, approved prescription. If your pet's prescription expires, the subscription is paused and you are notified to renew it.

### Managing Your Subscription

- Log into your account to view your active subscriptions.
- You can change the frequency, pause, or cancel at any time.
- Cancelling a subscription stops all future orders but does not affect orders already placed.

## Prescription Products: How the Rx Hold Works

When you add a prescription item to your cart, the checkout process includes an important safety step called the **prescription hold**. Here is exactly what happens and why:

### Step by Step

1. **Add to Cart:** You add the prescription item to your cart. The website asks you to associate this item with one of your pets on file. This is required — we need to know which pet the medication is for.

2. **Checkout:** You complete the checkout process as normal, selecting your fulfilment method and payment.

3. **Prescription Hold:** After checkout, your order status shows **"Awaiting Prescription."** This is normal and expected — it means your order is in the queue for veterinary review. No medication will be dispensed until a veterinarian approves it.

4. **Veterinary Review:** A veterinarian reviews your order alongside your pet's medical record. They check that:
   - The medication is appropriate for your pet's condition and species.
   - The dosage and formulation are correct.
   - Your pet has been examined within the required timeframe.
   - There is a valid veterinarian-client-patient relationship on file.

5. **Decision:**
   - **Approved:** The order moves to payment capture and fulfilment. You receive a confirmation.
   - **Rejected:** The order is cancelled and any payment is refunded. You receive a notification explaining the reason. Common reasons include the pet needing a more recent examination, an alternative treatment being more appropriate, or the medication being contraindicated.
   - **Queried:** The veterinarian may contact you with questions before making a decision. Responding promptly helps keep your order moving.

### How Long It Takes

- Prescription reviews are typically completed within **one business day**.
- If you need medication urgently, please call the clinic directly — the website is designed for routine refills, not emergency dispensing.

### Why This System Exists

Prescription medications are powerful. Dispensing the wrong medication, the wrong dose, or medication to the wrong pet can cause serious harm. The prescription hold ensures that a veterinarian who knows your pet reviews every prescription order. Safety comes before speed — always.
